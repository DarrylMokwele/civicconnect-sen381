# Forward Engineering Considerations (Milestone 1 Baseline)

| ID | Engineering Concern | Why It Matters Now | Later Decision / Activity Influenced | Missing Information / Trigger to Decide | Risk of Ignoring It |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **FEC-01** | **Automated Testing & CI Strategy** | Verification requirements dictate modular code structure and test harnesses. | Choice of test runners, CI build agents (GitHub Actions), and branch merge gates in M2/M3. | Final architectural layer separation and interface boundaries. | High maintenance overhead, untestable monolithic code, and high regression risk during milestone integration. |
| **FEC-02** | **Citizen PII & Security Governance (POPIA)** | Civic incident reports contain location, citizen names, and photographic evidence. | Data encryption at rest/in transit, database column hashing, and role-based access control (RBAC). | Specific municipal data classification standards and hosting compliance requirements. | Regulatory non-compliance penalties, citizen data breaches, and severe system redesign late in development. |
| **FEC-03** | **Observability, Auditing & Logging** | Civic issue status transitions require strict traceability to audit municipal response times. | Centralized logging framework, structured telemetry, and immutable audit trails. | Storage quota limits and log retention policies for public administration data. | Inability to diagnose field bugs or verify whether municipal SLA thresholds were breached. |
| **FEC-04** | **Deployment Target & Hosting Costs** | Hosting choices dictate containerization needs, cold-start latency, and runtime dependencies. | Cloud vs. on-premises deployment, container runtime (Docker), and cloud provider selection (Azure/AWS). | Client budget limits and institutional hosting environments. | Developing services that cannot run within resource, compute, or budget constraints during Milestone 3. |
| **FEC-05** | **Media Storage & Geolocation Processing** | Photo evidence attached to issue reports requires substantial bandwidth and storage. | Blob storage architecture (e.g., S3/Azure Blob vs. filesystem) and CDN integration. | Estimated citizen ticket upload volume and maximum attachment size constraints. | Exhausted database storage, slow page rendering, and high egress infrastructure costs. |
