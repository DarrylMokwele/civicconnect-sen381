# CivicConnect Problem and Business Need

## Problem Statement

Community service requests are currently received through email, phone calls, WhatsApp, spreadsheets and paper records. Because these channels are separate, requests can be duplicated, missed, assigned incorrectly or lost.

Requesters cannot easily see what is happening with their requests. Staff may struggle to see who is responsible for a request or which requests need attention. Management also lacks one reliable view of open, overdue and completed work.

## Business Need

CivicConnect is needed to keep service requests in one system. Requesters need to submit and track requests. Staff need to manage and update them. Management needs reliable information to monitor service delivery.

The system should also make it clear who is responsible for each request and what actions were taken.
